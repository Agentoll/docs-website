About

What Agentoll is

Monetize automated traffic on your site. Humans browse free; bots and AI agents pay a toll you set—in USDC on Base.

The problem

Crawlers and agents consume your content and API capacity at scale, without paying. Agentoll turns that traffic into revenue instead of cost.

What you get
