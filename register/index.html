    FAQ
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Is CNAME safe?
    
        
    Yes — only the hostname you point (e.g. www). Remove the CNAME to roll back.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Do you need our DNS password?
    
        
    No. You add one CNAME at your DNS provider.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Does curl get blocked before CNAME?
    
        
    HTTP 402 on your public URL requires CNAME + SSL. The script still works on pages where it is embedded.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    Payments and x402 settlement are handled by Agent Toll — no facilitator setup on your side.
